# NNNN — Short imperative title

- **Status:** proposed | accepted | superseded by NNNN | deprecated
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD
- **Supersedes:** NNNN (omit if none)

## Context

What made this a decision rather than an obvious call. The forces in tension — what
pulls one way, what pulls the other. Enough that someone reading in six months knows
why it wasn't trivial. Include the evidence that prompted it: numbers, file paths,
audit finding IDs.

## Decision

What was chosen, stated actively. "We will ..." One paragraph is usually enough. If it
takes three, the decision may actually be several decisions.

## Consequences

What becomes easier. What becomes harder. Be honest about the second part.

**Depends on:** the conditions that must remain true for this decision to keep holding.
If a filter depends on a column existing, say so here. This section is the one that
would have caught A6, and it is the reason this template exists.
